# Team rehearsal procedure

Suggested training schedule, not the official competition timetable.

1. **Setup, 15 minutes:** choose permitted past challenges; start isolated labs; confirm every teammate has the references locally. Assign primary and backup roles.
2. **Triage, 10 minutes:** survey challenges, estimate effort, assign an owner and one likely first test to each.
3. **Solve, 90 minutes:** use no AI assistance. Log evidence, update the board and hand off after 30 minutes without progress. Keep one submission owner.
4. **Recovery drill, 10 minutes:** disconnect internet or restart a VM safely; check that notes, tools and saved work remain usable.
5. **Debrief, 20 minutes:** explain the first useful observation, failed hypothesis and next skill to practise. Separate independent solves from assisted ones.
6. **Repeat later:** solve one missed challenge from a clean start without a walkthrough; have another teammate explain the mechanism.

Use [runs/README.md](../runs/README.md) for recording conventions. Keep unpublished challenge data and flags private. Suggested measurements: independent solves, time to first useful observation, blocked minutes, successful handoffs and ability to reproduce a solve.
